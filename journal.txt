[Day 1 · 3:00 PM]

I walked the stream this afternoon, thirty paces east of where the tent is staked. The water moves quick and clear over smooth stones — no stagnant pools, no smell, no color. Cold enough to ache my hand. I drank from it cupped, waited, watched the light shift through the pines. No ill feeling. I trust it now.

There's a shallowing just downstream where the bank flattens and the stones are smooth — a good place to fill a vessel without stirring silt. I'll work from that spot going forward.

One thing settled. A tent and a tested stream. Not nothing, for a first afternoon.
