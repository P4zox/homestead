# today.md — today's actions, in detail (cleared each new morning)
06:19 — First Fire, New Morning: woke in dark cabin — both lamps spent from the night before, only deep coals under pale ash keeping any warmth; raked ash aside to find five good orange coals still alive; laid dry grass over them, a strip of birch bark, blew gently — bark caught with a soft pop, light filling the hearthstones for the first time all night; added two birch splits; fire steady by 06:25; sat on sleeping mat watching the flame; frost hard outside, bench planks white; Day 98 begun in cold quiet.
07:19 — Two Lamps, Two Wicks: brought both spent bark-cup lamps to the hearthstones; opened first tallow envelope; worked fresh dry-grass wicks into each cup; lit first lamp from birch strip off the fire, lit second from the first — both burning steady; cabin lit again.
08:19 — Frost Line, One Trout: walked upstream in hard frost; ice finger-thick at outer bend; upstream line pulled — one fat bronze trout, still fighting; cleaned at hearthstones, wrapped in dock leaf, added to cold box; upstream hook re-baited with fresh stonefly larva (larvae now ~11); sixteen trout in cold box.
09:18 — South Snare Circuit: walked the south treeline in hard frost; snare had caught — one good tawny-grey rabbit, well-furred; reset snare with fresh two-ply cord; dressed rabbit at hearthstones, jointed meat dock-wrapped to cold box, pelt hung flesh-side out on east wall peg to stretch; water on for broth.
10:18 — Broth, Slow Morning: turned to the simmering water; added rabbit neck and trimmings, smoked hen-of-the-woods slices, a pinch of rose hip paste, handful of watercress — broth simmered dark and fragrant; ate the whole pot at the doorsill bench, frost still in the lean-to shadow, clearing gold and still; rose hip paste envelope spent.
11:18 — Acorn Cakes on Stone: opened one acorn flour envelope; mixed dough on grinding stone with water; shaped six small rounds on hearthstone beside coals; baked ~20 minutes, turned once; ate one warm, sealed five in bark envelope on supply shelf.
12:18 — Scraping the Pelt: took down the morning rabbit pelt; scraped flesh side flat on doorsill bench — thin membrane and fat curls removed; pelt re-hung on east wall peg, noticeably more supple; noon clearing still, frost in lean-to shadow gone, jay crossed once; ate one acorn cake at the door.
13:18 — Fat Into Fur: took down the morning rabbit pelt at 1:18 — edges stiffened, surface moisture gone; fat-worked flesh side with a small portion from the tallow envelope, slow circles, an hour of work; pelt re-hung flesh side in on east wall peg, pliable and waxy; tallow envelope returned to supply shelf, still usable.
14:19 — Second Log Check: walked north slope to check buried log — mycelium face white and alive but no second flush yet, soil cool and damp not frozen; reset weight stones; estimated Day 103–104 for second flush; gathered small double-handful of fresh wood ears from alder bend above east seep on return; back at cabin before afternoon light dropped.
