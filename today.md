# today.md — today's actions, in detail (cleared each new morning)

06:05 — Unlocked shelter at first light; walked to stream; found trout on overnight line in mossy cleft; cleaned at bank; held trout in cold-storage box for smoking when rack is ready; re-baited hook and reset line; kindled fire from coals; set water boiling; sat by fire with axe, planning the day's smoke rack work — four forked uprights to cut and drive south of shelter.
07:05 — Went to forest margin and clearing edge; cut four Y-forked birch saplings for smoke-rack uprights; trimmed shanks to two hand-spans below fork; drove all four into ground south of shelter in two paired sets — forks cupping upward; rack frame ready to receive ridgepole and crossbars.
08:05 — Selected a straight pine offcut for ridgepole; lifted it into forked uprights; soaked six bark-cord coils in water tin; lashed ridgepole at both fork-pairs with crossing turns; cut three birch crossbars from sapling trimmings and lashed them horizontally between the upright pairs in ascending steps — six cords, six lashing points, all used; pushed ridgepole hard to test — rack held as a unit; smoke rack fully complete; trout waiting in cold box for smoking this afternoon.
09:05 — Retrieved trout from cold-storage box; looped bark cord through tail and hung fish from smoke-rack ridgepole above second crossbar; banked fire low with coals, laid green birch boughs over top; thick white birch smoke rising steadily through rack and folding around fish — first smoking begun; settled nearby on split pine round to tend fire, keeping flame suppressed and smoke steady; trout to cure at least through noon.
10:04 — Tended smoking fire; added green birch boughs to coal bed; walked to stream for more branches; noticed and tested exposed clay layer at stream bank bend — gray-blue, holds shape, workable; ~10–15 paces of good clay face; noted as future clay-hearth source; trout bronzing on rack, smoke steady.
11:04 — Tended smoking fire; walked arc around rack and inspected trout — skin bronzed and darkened, no longer raw; rotated fish a quarter-turn on cord so other side faces heavier smoke; laid fresh green birch boughs over coals to maintain thick white plume; thinking ahead to clay harvest from stream-bank seep this afternoon for indoor hearth.
12:05 — Checked trout at noon: skin bronzed and firm, shoulder still yields; extended smoking to ~14:00 for fuller cure; added green birch boughs; walked to clay bank and scraped cook tin full of gray-blue clay from exposed face at stream bend; carried clay back, set in shade at shelter entrance for hearth shaping this afternoon.
