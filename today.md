# today.md — today's actions, in detail (cleared each new morning)

06:04 — Woke at first light to a cool, still morning; walked to the stream and checked the fishing line — hook bare, bait taken in the night; found a fat grub under a stone near the bank, re-baited and reset the line in the mossy cleft; stood a while at the water watching the current, then turned back toward the shelter; east slope still needs six to eight more bark courses to the ridge — that is today's first task.
07:04 — Headed to the birch stand at east clearing edge; harvested nine large bark sheets from trees already started and a pair of untouched wrist-thick birches; rolled and hauled sheets to shelter site; foraged a small fistful of wild garlic along clearing edge on the way back.
08:05 — Worked nine staged bark sheets onto east slope from last sealed course up to ridgepole; lapped and lashed each sheet to purlins; finished with a ridge-cap half-sheet over both slopes; east face sealed eave to ridge — confirmed by hand: dark inside.
09:04 — Walked the clearing margin east and north with intent; cut an armful of nettles from a stand at the foot of a fallen pine and along the stream bank; dug dandelion roots at the clearing edge; added more wild garlic from the birch stand; boiled a full cook-tin of green soup (nettles, dandelion root, wild garlic) and ate it — first real meal in two days; fishing line still set.
10:04 — Walked west clearing edge; found three untouched birch in a stand forty meters out; peeled six large bark sheets from two trees; hauled to shelter and laid flat near west-slope base — west slope bark work begun; will check fishing line at noon.
11:04 — Lifted six staged bark sheets onto west slope; lashed two full eave courses from left to right — bark-side up, cord-wrapped to purlins, overlapping eave; west slope eave sealed, upper slope still open lattice; fishing line still set for noon check.
12:04 — Checked fishing line at noon: caught a small trout (palm-sized, taken on grub bait — first fish); cleaned at stream bank, cooked on green-stick frame over coals, ate well; re-baited and reset line in mossy cleft for afternoon.
