# today.md — today's actions, in detail (cleared each new morning)
06:18 — Rowans to the Smoke: rose at first light with frost on the clearing grass; broke last coals to ember and fed thin birch splits until fire caught bright; moved twelve rowan clusters from lean-to crossbar to smoke rack — four cord loops laid over ridgepole so clusters hang free above the birch-smoke fire; smoke rising steady and white in still cold air; five bark envelopes firm and cured on hearth edge, moved to supply shelf.
07:00 — Snare Walk South: walked south slope in hard frost, breath clouding in still cold air; first snare empty but bait taken — re-baited and reset; second snare sprung and empty — reset; third snare at fallen birch log: wood pigeon caught clean overnight, heavy and still warm; dressed bird at chopping block, added to cold box in dock leaves; rowan clusters beginning to wrinkle on smoke rack.
08:19 — Smoke-Curing the Hide: lashed rabbit hide to green birch hoop, hung over smoke below rowans; turned front and back each quarter-hour; color shifted pale cream to warm honey tan — hide smoke-finished, supple as cloth; folded and on supply shelf.
09:19 — Upstream in the Cold: walked the stream to check the upstream line (set Day 77 12:00); two brook trout on three hooks — third bait taken clean; reset line with fresh grubs; trout added to cold box in dock leaves (nine trout now in box); checked drying crock in north lean-to — no new cracks, rim rings true, still faintly cool inside — not yet ready for firing; rowans continuing to smoke on rack.
10:18 — First Acorn Porridge: spooned half an envelope of ground acorn flour into clay pot with cold stream water; cooked over good coal bed with birch spoon; added dried mint; ate warm from wide shallow bowl — mild, nutty, faint tannin edge, satisfying; one full envelope + partial remain on shelf; rowans still smoking on rack.
11:18 — Rowans Off the Smoke: twelve clusters pulled from smoke rack after five hours of birch smoke; berries darkened rust-brown and dry to the center; stripped from stems, pressed into two bark envelopes, resin-sealed, added to supply shelf beside elderberry reduction; rack empty, fire let down to coals.
12:19 — Sweet from Sour: brought twenty dock-wrapped crab apples from cold box (softened and mellowed after two days); halved and cored at chopping block; simmered in clay pot over rowan-smoke coals with a finger of stream water; stirred with birch spoon until flesh collapsed; cooked down to thick tart-sweet dark-pink paste; sealed in bark envelope with pine resin while warm — on supply shelf; crab apple stock spent.
